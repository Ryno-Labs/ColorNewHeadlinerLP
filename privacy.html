Privacy & Contact

For the main Get Upholstery Quotes website at getupholsteryquotes.com. Updated September 2026.

Who operates the site?

Seek & Scale operates Get Upholstery Quotes as an independent marketing and quote service. Seek & Scale does not perform the upholstery repairs. An active campaign page identifies the business that will perform its service.

What does this website collect?

The main site has no quote form, user account, advertising pixel, or site-set tracking cookie. It links to individual service pages. GitHub Pages, the hosting platform, may process basic technical information such as your IP address to serve the website. This main page uses local image and logo files; it does not load external analytics or ad scripts.

What happens when you ask for a quote?

On an active service page, you may choose to open your phone's messaging app. If you send a message, we receive your phone number and anything you share, such as a vehicle model or upholstery problem. The messaging app and mobile carrier handle the message according to their own practices. Seek & Scale may answer the quote conversation and coordinate with the named service provider to price or schedule the work.

Will you send my details to another shop?

We may share relevant quote details with the provider identified on the active campaign page to respond to your request. If a different provider is proposed, we will tell you and ask before sharing your details with that provider. We do not use the main page to enroll you in promotional texts.

Keeping and deleting messages

Quote messages may be kept as needed to handle requests, scheduling, normal business records, and legal duties. You may ask about or request deletion of information you shared. Some records may need to be retained for legal or business reasons.

Questions or privacy requests?

Text 512-931-1752 or call 512-931-1752 and ask for Seek & Scale about Get Upholstery Quotes.

If quote forms, analytics, advertising pixels, automation, or new partner routing are added, this policy and the relevant campaign disclosures must be updated.
