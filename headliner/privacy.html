Privacy & Contact

Applies to the headliner quote page at getupholsteryquotes.com/headliner/.

Who runs this page?

Seek and Scale manages this quote page for Color New Upholstery. Color New Upholstery in Georgetown, Texas performs the headliner service.

What happens when you request a quote?

The headliner page uses a short online form. When you submit it, the information is sent by email to ryanclark.autointeriors@gmail.com so Seek and Scale can manage the quote request for Color New Upholstery. Your request and contact details may be provided to Color New Upholstery so it can respond about the service you asked for.

What information is used?

The form collects the vehicle year, make and model, whether it has a sunroof, the reason for replacement, your name, and your mobile number. We use this information to handle your headliner quote request and help Color New Upholstery respond to you.

Who gets your information?

Seek and Scale and Color New Upholstery may use your quote information to price, schedule, and complete the Color New service. If you check the optional text-message consent box, Color New Upholstery may text you about this quote request. The form does not sign you up for promotional texts.

Site data and tools

This page uses FormSubmit to process the quote form and deliver submissions by email. FormSubmit states that it retains form submissions for 30 days. GitHub Pages, the site host, may handle basic technical information needed to serve the page, such as your IP address. This page does not currently use an advertising pixel, analytics script, or site-set tracking cookie.

How long is the conversation kept?

Quote requests and related business records may be kept as needed to handle the request, maintain business records, and meet legal duties. You can ask about your information or request deletion. Some records may need to be kept for business or legal reasons.

Questions or privacy requests?

Email ryanclark.autointeriors@gmail.com or call 512-931-1752 and ask about Get Upholstery Quotes.
